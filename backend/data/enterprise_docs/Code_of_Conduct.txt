NOVATECH SOLUTIONS — INTERNAL ENTERPRISE POLICY
Document: Code_of_Conduct.txt
Version: 4.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Audience: All Directors, Officers, and Employees

SECTION 1: WORKPLACE RESPECT & INCLUSIVITY
1.1 Zero-Tolerance Anti-Harassment:
NovaTech Solutions is committed to providing a professional, respectful work environment free from discrimination, sexual harassment, bullying, and intimidation. Any violation leads to immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

1.2 Diversity and Equal Opportunity:
All hiring, promotions, and compensation decisions are based strictly on merit, performance, and qualifications without regard to race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age.

SECTION 2: CONFLICT OF INTEREST & GIFTS
2.1 Outside Business Activities:
Employees must not engage in external commercial activities or consulting that compete with NovaTech Solutions or create a conflict of interest. Any outside employment or directorship must be declared in writing and approved by the Legal & Compliance Department.

2.2 Corporate Gifts:
Employees may accept nominal promotional gifts from clients or vendors valued at under $50. Any gift or hospitality exceeding $50 must be declined or declared to the Compliance Officer. Under no circumstances may cash or cash equivalents (gift cards) be accepted.

SECTION 3: WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION & REPORTING
3.1 Confidential Ethics Hotline:
Employees who observe illegal conduct, accounting fraud, or safety violations may report anonymously via the NovaTech Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-ETHIC or ethics@novatech.com).

3.2 Non-Retaliation Guarantee:
NovaTech strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who in good faith reports an actual or suspected violation of this Code.
