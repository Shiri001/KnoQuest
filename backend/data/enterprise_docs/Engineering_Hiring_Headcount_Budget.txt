NOVATECH ENGINEERING HIRING AND HEADCOUNT BUDGET POLICY
Document ID: POL-ENG-HC-2026-V1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: Engineering Directors, Hiring Managers, and Recruiting Leads
Classification: Tier 2 - Management & Leadership (Restricted to Managers & Executives)

SECTION 1: FY26 HEADCOUNT ALLOCATION MATRIX
The Engineering department is authorized for forty-two (42) new full-time requisitions across FY26, allocated as follows:
- Platform & Cloud Infrastructure: 14 openings (Kubernetes, Terraform, Azure RAG specialist roles).
- Core AI & Search Agents: 16 openings (FastAPI, LLM orchestration, vector databases).
- Enterprise Web & Mobile Frontend: 8 openings (React 19, TypeScript, WCAG accessibility).
- Security & Compliance: 4 openings (DevSecOps, penetration testing, IAM).

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION BANDS BY LEVEL (CONFIDENTIAL TO HIRING MANAGERS)
- Level L3 (Associate Software Engineer): Base $95,000 - $115,000 | Stock Grant: $20,000 over 4 yrs.
- Level L4 (Software Engineer II): Base $125,000 - $150,000 | Stock Grant: $45,000 over 4 yrs.
- Level L5 (Senior Software Engineer): Base $160,000 - $190,000 | Stock Grant: $90,000 over 4 yrs.
- Level L6 (Staff / Principal Engineer): Base $200,000 - $240,000 | Stock Grant: $160,000 over 4 yrs.
- Level M1/M2 (Engineering Manager / Director): Base $195,000 - $250,000 | Target Bonus: 25% | Stock Grant: $180,000 over 4 yrs.

SECTION 3: OFFER EXTENSION APPROVAL MATRIX
Offers within the midpoint range require approval from the Hiring Manager and HR Talent Partner. Any offer exceeding the 75th percentile of the band or requesting sign-on bonuses over $15,000 requires direct written approval from the VP of Engineering and CFO.
