NOVATECH SOLUTIONS — INTERNAL ENTERPRISE POLICY
Document: IT_Security_Policy.txt
Version: 5.1 | Effective Date: January 15, 2025
Audience: All Employees, Contractors, and Third-Party Associates

SECTION 1: PASSWORD STANDARDS & AUTHENTICATION
1.1 Minimum Length and Complexity:
All passwords for enterprise accounts (Active Directory, Microsoft 365, Cloud Portals) must be a minimum of twelve (12) characters in length. Passwords must combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols (e.g., !@#$%^&*).

1.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory for all employees across all enterprise portals, email accounts, and remote access systems. Hardware tokens or Microsoft Authenticator app push notifications must be used.

1.3 Password Expiration & Prohibitions:
Passwords expire every ninety (90) days. Password reuse of the previous six (6) passwords is prevented. Sharing passwords with colleagues or writing them on notes is a disciplinary offense.

SECTION 2: INCIDENT REPORTING & TIMELINE
2.1 Mandatory 30-Minute Reporting:
Any suspected security incident, including phishing attempts, malware infections, unauthorized access attempts, or lost/stolen hardware, MUST be reported to the IT Security Operations Center (soc@novatech.com or extension 4444) within thirty (30) minutes of discovery.

SECTION 3: DATA HANDLING & STORAGE RESTRICTIONS
3.1 Confidentiality & Personal Cloud Storage:
Confidential and proprietary NovaTech information must NEVER be uploaded, synced, or backed up to personal cloud storage services such as personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, or personal OneDrive. All company documents must reside on official NovaTech SharePoint or OneDrive enterprise repositories.

3.2 USB and Removable Media:
USB mass storage devices are blocked by default on all company workstations via Endpoint DLP. Any business need for USB data transfer requires written authorization from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
