NOVATECH SOLUTIONS — INTERNAL ENTERPRISE POLICY
Document: Leave_Holiday_Policy.txt
Version: 4.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Audience: All NovaTech Employees

SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE (PAID TIME OFF)
1.1 Entitlement:
Full-time confirmed employees are entitled to twenty (20) days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues on a pro-rata basis of 1.67 days per completed month of service.

1.2 Carry Forward:
Employees may carry forward a maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Any carried forward days must be utilized before March 31 of that year, otherwise they lapse.

1.3 Application & Approval:
Leave requests exceeding three (3) consecutive days must be submitted through the HR portal at least two (2) weeks in advance and require direct manager approval.

SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE & MEDICAL LEAVE
2.1 Allocation:
Employees receive twelve (12) days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave is credited on January 1 of each year.

2.2 Medical Certificate Requirement:
For any medical absence lasting two (2) or more consecutive days, an authentic medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner must be uploaded to the HR portal within 48 hours of resuming work.

SECTION 3: PARENTAL LEAVE
3.1 Maternity Leave:
Eligible female employees receive twenty-six (26) weeks of fully paid maternity leave.
3.2 Paternity Leave:
Eligible male employees receive two (2) weeks (10 working days) of paid paternity leave, to be taken within six months of the child's birth.

SECTION 4: PUBLIC HOLIDAYS & BEREAVEMENT
4.1 Public Holidays:
NovaTech Solutions observes ten (10) declared mandatory public holidays and two (2) optional floating holidays per year.
4.2 Bereavement Leave:
Up to five (5) consecutive working days of paid leave is provided in the unfortunate event of the death of an immediate family member.
