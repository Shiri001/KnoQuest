NOVATECH SOLUTIONS — INTERNAL ENTERPRISE POLICY
Document: Travel_Expense_Policy.txt
Version: 3.4 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Audience: All Employees Traveling on Official Business

SECTION 1: TRAVEL AUTHORIZATION & FLIGHT BOOKINGS
1.1 Prior Approval:
All domestic travel must be pre-approved by the department director at least 7 days in advance. International business travel requires pre-approval from the Vice President at least 21 days in advance.

1.2 Air Travel:
Economy class is mandatory for all domestic flights and short-haul international flights under six (6) hours duration. Premium economy or business class is permitted only for international flights exceeding eight (8) continuous hours.

SECTION 2: HOTEL ACCOMMODATION & PER DIEM
2.1 Hotel Reimbursement Cap:
The maximum domestic hotel reimbursement is $150 per night (excluding taxes). For Tier 1 metropolitan areas (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London), the cap is adjusted up to $220 per night. Itemized hotel folios must be attached to the expense claim.

2.2 Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem):
Employees are provided a daily meal and incidental per diem allowance of $60 per day during business travel. Receipts for individual meals under $25 are not required if within the per diem limit, but alcoholic beverages are strictly non-reimbursable.

SECTION 3: GROUND TRANSPORTATION & EXPENSE REPORTING
3.1 Rideshare and Public Transit:
Use of ridesharing services (Uber, Lyft, local taxi) or public transit for travel between airports, hotels, and client sites is fully reimbursable. Personal mileage for business travel is reimbursed at the standard rate of $0.67 per mile.

3.2 Submission Deadline:
Expense reports accompanied by all itemized receipts must be submitted via the NovaTech Financial Portal within fourteen (14) calendar days of trip conclusion.
