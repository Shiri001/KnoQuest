NOVATECH SOLUTIONS — INTERNAL ENTERPRISE POLICY
Document: Employee_Benefits.txt
Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Audience: Confirmed Full-Time NovaTech Employees

SECTION 1: HEALTHCARE & INSURANCE
1.1 Comprehensive Health Coverage:
NovaTech Solutions provides full-time employees and their immediate dependents (spouse and up to two children) with comprehensive health insurance coverage up to $50,000 per policy year.

1.2 Dental and Vision Care:
The plan covers annual dental cleanings and up to $500 per year in preventive dental procedures. An annual vision allowance of $250 is provided for prescription eyewear or eye examinations.

1.3 Mental Health & Wellness (EAP):
The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers up to six (6) confidential, free counseling sessions per year with certified wellness counselors.

SECTION 2: RETIREMENT & FINANCIAL WELLBEING
2.1 401(k) / Provident Fund Match:
NovaTech matches employee retirement contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 5% of the employee's base annual salary, vesting immediately upon completion of one full year of service.

SECTION 3: PROFESSIONAL LEARNING & TUITION ASSISTANCE
3.1 Annual Learning Stipend:
Each confirmed full-time employee receives an annual professional development allowance of up to $1,500. This fund can be used for industry certifications (e.g., Azure, AWS, PMP), technical conferences, and accredited academic courses.
