NOVATECH MANAGEMENT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PERFORMANCE CALIBRATION
Document ID: SOP-MGT-PERF-2026-V1
Effective Date: January 10, 2026
Applicability: People Managers, Engineering Directors, and Executive Leadership
Classification: Tier 2 - Management & Leadership (Restricted to Managers & Executives)

SECTION 1: ANNUAL & MID-YEAR REVIEW CADENCE
People managers must conduct bi-annual performance calibrations across all direct reports. Mid-year check-ins occur in June, followed by annual review cycles concluding each December. Ratings determine merit base salary adjustments, equity refresher grants, and promotional velocity.

SECTION 2: THE 9-BOX TALENT CALIBRATION MATRIX
Managers must assess team members across two dimensions: Performance (Low, Medium, High) and Potential (Low, Medium, High):
1. Box 9 (High Performance, High Potential): Fast-track candidates eligible for leadership mentorship, top-quartile bonus allocations (120-150% of target), and expedited promotion cycles.
2. Box 5 (Medium Performance, Medium Potential): Core steady contributors constituting approximately 50% of the team. Eligible for standard 100% target bonus and standard merit increments.
3. Box 1 (Low Performance, Low Potential): Individuals requiring immediate structured coaching or formal Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs).

SECTION 3: FORCED DISTRIBUTION & BUDGET CALIBRATION
To maintain equitable appraisal rigor, department directors must normalize evaluations across teams according to standard distribution targets: Top Tier (15-20%), Core Contributors (70-75%), and Underperforming / Needs Improvement (5-10%). Merit budgets cannot exceed 4.5% of departmental base payroll without Chief Financial Officer sign-off.

SECTION 4: DELIVERING CRITICAL FEEDBACK AND RATING DISCLOSURE
Ratings must be communicated during 1-on-1 private sessions only after formal Departmental Calibration Committee approval. Managers must provide clear, actionable milestone documentation for any rating below "Meets Expectations".
