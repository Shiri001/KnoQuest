NOVATECH ENTERPRISE WORKPLACE HEALTH AND SAFETY POLICY
Document ID: POL-WHS-2026-V3
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: All Global Employees, Contractors, and On-Site Visitors
Classification: Tier 1 - General Enterprise (All Staff)

SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND CORE COMMITMENT
NovaTech is dedicated to maintaining a safe, healthy, and hazard-free work environment across all corporate facilities, regional innovation hubs, and remote home office environments. This policy sets forth compliance mandates in accordance with OSHA standards and international occupational health guidelines.

SECTION 2: ERGONOMIC WORKSTATION STANDARDS
All employees are entitled to an ergonomic workplace evaluation within thirty (30) days of hire or relocation. NovaTech provides adjustable ergonomic chairs, height-adjustable standing converter desks, monitor riser arms, and anti-fatigue floor mats upon request through the Facilities Management portal. Remote employees may claim an annual $300 ergonomic equipment stipend through Concur expense reporting with manager sign-off.

SECTION 3: EMERGENCY EVACUATION AND FIRE SAFETY
1. Evacuation Drills: Emergency evacuation drills are conducted semi-annually across all physical offices. All occupants must promptly exit via designated stairwells upon hearing audible alarms. Elevators are automatically locked out.
2. Assembly Points: Each facility designates primary and secondary assembly muster points located at least 150 feet away from the structure. Floor wardens conduct headcount verification using automated RFID badge scanner rosters.
3. First Aid & AED Units: Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) and certified first-aid trauma kits are stationed adjacent to elevators on every floor. Certified first responders are listed on floor safety noticeboards.

SECTION 4: ACCIDENT AND INCIDENT REPORTING
Any workplace injury, illness, slip/trip incident, or near-miss hazard must be reported to People Operations and Facilities within twenty-four (24) hours using the internal Health & Safety Incident Form. Emergency medical incidents must be escalated immediately to local emergency services (911/112) and Security Dispatch extension 4444.

SECTION 5: SUBSTANCE-FREE WORKPLACE AND COMPLIANCE
NovaTech enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding the possession, distribution, or influence of illicit drugs, unprescribed controlled substances, or alcohol during core working hours and on corporate premises. Violations will result in disciplinary review up to and including immediate termination of employment.
