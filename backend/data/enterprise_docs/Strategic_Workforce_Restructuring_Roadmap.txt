SECTION 1: AI AUTOMATION IMPACT ON INTERNAL OPERATIONS
Over the 2026-2028 horizon, the deployment of NovaTech autonomous agents is projected to automate 40% of Tier-1 helpdesk ticketing, routine accounts payable invoice processing, and standard contract review workflows.

SECTION 2: RESKILLING AND REALLOCATION FRAMEWORK
1. Transition Academy: A dedicated $4,000,000 corporate upskilling academy will transition affected frontline personnel into high-value roles, including AI Agent Prompt Engineering, Enterprise Client Success, and Data Annotation Quality Assurance.
2. Natural Attrition: Frontline staffing reductions will be achieved primarily through targeted natural attrition (projected at 6.5% annually).

SECTION 3: SEVERANCE AND OUTPLACEMENT RESERVES
In the event involuntary redundancies are mandated in legacy support units in Q4 2026, an initial severance reserve of $6,500,000 has been set aside, providing affected staff with minimum twelve (12) weeks severance, accelerated health coverage, and external outplacement career coaching.
