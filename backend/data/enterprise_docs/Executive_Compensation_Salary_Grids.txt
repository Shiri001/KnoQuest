SECTION 2: LONG-TERM INCENTIVE PLAN (LTIP) VESTING SCHEDULE
Executive equity awards vest on a four-year schedule with a one-year cliff (25% vesting after 12 months, followed by 6.25% quarterly vesting thereafter). Performance Share Units (PSUs) are tied to 3-year Total Shareholder Return (TSR) metrics and recurring SaaS annual growth targets.

SECTION 3: SEVERANCE AND GOLDEN PARACHUTE PROVISIONS
Executive employment agreements stipulate twelve (12) months base salary continuation, accelerated single-trigger equity vesting of 50% upon a Change of Control event, and 18 months of executive COBRA healthcare coverage.
