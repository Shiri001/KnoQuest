NOVATECH SOLUTIONS — INTERNAL ENTERPRISE POLICY
Document: HR_Policy.txt
Version: 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Audience: All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees

SECTION 1: WORKING HOURS & SCHEDULE
1.1 Standard Office Hours:
NovaTech Solutions operates on standard business hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours during which all employees must be reachable are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

1.2 Attendance and Punctuality:
Employees are expected to log their presence using the NovaTech intranet portal. Punctuality is essential for team coordination and client delivery.

SECTION 2: PROBATION PERIOD
2.1 Duration:
All newly hired employees undergo a mandatory probationary period of six (6) months from their official start date.

2.2 Performance Review & Confirmation:
A formal probation evaluation takes place at the end of month 5 with the department manager. Upon successful review, employment is confirmed in writing. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months if performance criteria are partially met.

SECTION 3: EMPLOYEE CLASSIFICATIONS
3.1 Full-Time Regular: Employees working 40 hours per week, eligible for all company benefits and leave programs.
3.2 Contract Employees: Retained under fixed-term contracts; entitled to statutory benefits as defined in their engagement contract.

SECTION 4: TERMINATION & NOTICE PERIOD
4.1 Notice Period:
Confirmed full-time employees must provide thirty (30) calendar days' written notice upon resignation. During probation, the notice period is fourteen (14) calendar days.
