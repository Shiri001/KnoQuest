SECTION 1: EMERGENCY SUCCESSION ORDER OF PRECEDENCE
To guarantee unbroken operational continuity in the event of unexpected incapacitation or departure of key executive officers:
1. Chief Executive Officer (CEO):
   - Primary Interim Successor: Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
   - Secondary Interim Successor: Chief Technology Officer (CTO).
   - Board Action: Emergency meeting convened within 24 hours to ratify interim leadership and initiate executive search committee.
2. Chief Information Security Officer (CISO - David Kumar):
   - Primary Interim Successor: Marcus Vance (Lead IT Infrastructure Engineer).
   - Secondary Interim Successor: Director of Cloud Security Architecture.

SECTION 2: CRISIS DELEGATION OF FINANCIAL EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY
During declared corporate emergencies, expenditure authority of up to $25,000,000 is automatically delegated to the interim Chief Executive Officer with concurrent written concurrence from the Chair of the Board Audit Committee.

SECTION 3: SECURED BOARD ROSTER AND EMERGENCY CONTACT PROTOCOL
Encrypted satellite phones and off-grid Signal communication channels are provisioned for all seven (7) members of the Board of Directors with annual failover communication verifications.
